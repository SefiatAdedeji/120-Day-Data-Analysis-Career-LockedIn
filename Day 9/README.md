## Overview  

Today, I continued working on the Sales Performance Dashboard in Excel and completed the analytical phase of the project.

The focus was on:
- Year-over-Year (YoY) comparison
- Final KPI validation
- Conditional formatting for performance tracking
- Completing the dashboard layout

---

## Work Completed  

### 1. Year-over-Year (YoY) Analysis  

I calculated YoY changes for:

- Revenue  
- Orders  
- Quantity  
- Discount  

This allowed me to evaluate growth trends and performance shifts over time.

---

### 2. Conditional Formatting for Performance Indicators  

I applied conditional formatting across all YoY metrics:

- 🟢 Green → Positive growth  
- 🔴 Red → Negative change  
- ⚫ Black → Neutral / no significant change  

This improved visual interpretation and made performance trends immediately clear without needing long explanations.

---

### 3. Revenue Analysis Breakdown  

Revenue was analyzed across multiple dimensions:

- By Month  
- By Product Category  
- By Customer Segment  
- By State  

This provided a multi-level view of performance and highlighted where growth or decline is occurring.

---

### 4. Dashboard Layout Completed

- Clean layout and structured sections
